Prompting

To get exceptional responses, a good prompt must combine four key elements: Role (who the AI ​​is supposed to simulate), Context (the scenario or purpose), Task (the specific action to be performed), and Constraints (tone of voice, length, or formatting). The more specific you are, the more the AI ​​stops guessing.

Files and folders

A well-organized structure of files and folders makes it easier to find information, improves the efficiency of collaboration among team members, and leads to better results

  • Give files clear names that immediately identify their content, avoiding generic titles such as Document1.pdf.
  • Group documents into folders dedicated to specific projects, clients, or work areas to make them easier to find.
  • Avoid creating too many levels of subfolders, which can make it harder to find documents quickly.
  • Update the documents while following a versioning convention (for example, v1, v2, or using the date), so that the team is always working on the most recent version.
