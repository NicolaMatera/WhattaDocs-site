Whattadata is a spin-off of the University of Milan-Bicocca that transforms data management and analysis into concrete solutions for decision-making, processes, and services

Our Team

Whattadata was founded by the University of Milan-Bicocca to bring innovation to data management and analysis. We develop practical solutions that optimize decision-making processes and strategic services.

Whattadata bridges the gap between science and business. We leverage a company's information assets by developing customized solutions to simplify the management of day-to-day processes.
